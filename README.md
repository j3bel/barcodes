# Barcodes

Scan a barcode once, then show it on your phone at any scanner — loyalty cards,
gym tags, library cards, tickets.

**Open it:** https://j3bel.github.io/barcodes/

On iPhone: open the link in Safari → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Your cards are stored only on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Uses [ZXing](https://github.com/zxing-js/library) (Apache-2.0) to read barcodes
and [bwip-js](https://github.com/metafloor/bwip-js) (MIT) to draw them.
